Your plan

Aggressive cut playbook

  1. Lose about 1% of your body weight a week, 2 lb at most.Past that, more of what comes off is muscle instead of fat.
  2. Eat about 1 g of protein per pound of your goal weight.High protein is what protects muscle while calories are low.
  3. Lift 3 or more days a week and keep the weights heavy.Lifting is the signal that tells your body to hold on to muscle.
  4. Add 150 to 300 minutes of cardio a week.Walking counts. It widens the deficit without cutting more food.
  5. Weigh in most mornings and judge the weekly trend.A big drop in week one is mostly water, and daily numbers bounce.
  6. Hold the floor.This app won't set you under 1,500 calories a day, or 1,200 for women. Diets of 800 or less need a doctor: very fast loss raises the risk of gallstones and muscle loss.
  7. Ease off if you feel dizzy or weak, or your lifts keep dropping.Switch to Steady for a couple of weeks, then push again.

Where this comes from: MacroFactor on rates of weight loss, a review of protein while cutting, and the Obesity Action Coalition on crash-diet risks. General guidance, not medical advice.
